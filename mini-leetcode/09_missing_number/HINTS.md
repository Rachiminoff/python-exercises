# 💡 Hints — Missing Number

*Tags: array, math*

Try the problem for real first. These are ordered from vague to specific — stop reading as soon as you
see a way forward.

## Hints

1. The numbers 0..n should sum to n*(n+1)//2 — there's a closed-form formula, no loop needed for that part.
2. Compare that expected sum to the actual sum of the given list. The gap is your missing number.
