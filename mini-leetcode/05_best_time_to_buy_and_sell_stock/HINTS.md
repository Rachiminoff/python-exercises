# 💡 Hints — Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock

*Tags: array, single pass*

Try the problem for real first. These are ordered from vague to specific — stop reading as soon as you
see a way forward.

## Hints

1. Walk through the prices once, tracking the lowest price seen so far.
2. At each price, ask: what profit would I make selling today, given the lowest price so far?
3. Keep a running best-profit-seen-so-far as you go.
