# 💡 Hints — 🎵 No-Repeat Playlist Shuffler

Try the exercise for real first. These are ordered from vague to specific — stop reading as soon as you
see a way forward.

## Hints

1. random.shuffle(list) shuffles a list in place — shuffle a copy of your playlist once at the start.
2. Use list.pop() to pull and remove the next song from the shuffled copy each time.
3. When the shuffled copy is empty, reshuffle a fresh copy of the full playlist to keep going.

## Relevant functions & syntax

`random.shuffle()`, `list.pop()`, `list.copy()`, `input()`
