# 💡 Hints — 🎂 Birthday Countdown

Try the exercise for real first. These are ordered from vague to specific — stop reading as soon as you
see a way forward.

## Hints

1. datetime.date.today() gives you today's date.
2. Build this year's birthday as a date object; if it's already passed, bump it to next year with .replace(year=...).
3. Subtracting two date objects gives a timedelta — use .days to get a plain number.

## Relevant functions & syntax

`datetime.date`, `datetime.date.today()`, `date.replace()`, `timedelta.days`
