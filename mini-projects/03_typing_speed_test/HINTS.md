# 💡 Hints — ⌨️ Typing Speed Test

Try the exercise for real first. These are ordered from vague to specific — stop reading as soon as you
see a way forward.

## Hints

1. Call time.time() right before and right after input(), then subtract to get elapsed seconds.
2. WPM = (number of words in the sentence) / (elapsed seconds / 60).
3. For accuracy, compare characters pairwise with zip(original, typed) and count how many match.

## Relevant functions & syntax

`time.time()`, `str.split()`, `zip()`, `round()`
